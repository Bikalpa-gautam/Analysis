TRAGWERK STUDIO

A clear view of your structure.

Three workspaces, one place to build your model and understand its behaviour.

01

Define your model

Choose an example, then adjust geometry, sections and loads in Model setup. Linear results update as you edit.

02

Explore the response

Use the viewport controls to show forces, displacement or utilisation. In 3D, drag to orbit, right-drag to pan and scroll to zoom.

03

Inspect the details

Select a member or element to see its results. Open a design check to trace the equations, values and code references.
